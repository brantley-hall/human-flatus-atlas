The article covers how University of Maryland researchers have developed Smart Underwear to measure flatulence frequency, discovering that humans pass gas about 32 times per day - much higher than the traditional medical estimate of 14 times per day. The research reveals wide variation (4-59 times per day) with some individuals exceeding 100 times daily, challenging conventional medical wisdom about normal gas patterns.
